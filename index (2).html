Technology Controls & Compliance Dashboard

Power BI, DAX

Synthetic data

GitHubLive demo

I modeled control testing, access reviews and vendor risk assessments in Power BI, with DAX measures for exception rates, remediation aging and segregation-of-duties conflicts. Change Management is the weakest control category, and legacy on-prem systems fail at about twice the cloud rate (19.9% vs. 9.8%). A What-If model shows a 15-day access-review SLA would cut on-time completion from 77.8% to 46.3%.

Excel controls testing

Synthetic data

GitHub

The framework tests termination timeliness, segregation of duties, approvals and privileged-access recertification using INDEX/MATCH, COUNTIFS and nested IF. NYC 311 SLA Breach Analysis

Python, SQL, Power BI

Public data: NYC 311

GitHub

A Pandas to MySQL star-schema pipeline turned 854,221 raw requests into 853,689 validated records. Breach rates are driven by agency (3% NYPD vs. 98% Housing Preservation & Development) more than borough (35% to 48.5%), which moves the root cause from geography to agency process.

39.4%SLA breach rate on a 24-hour threshold
